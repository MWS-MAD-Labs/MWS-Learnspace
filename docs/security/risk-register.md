# Security and operational risk register

## Status rules

This register records unresolved P7-006/P7-007/P7-008 risks as of 2026-09-02. `Open` means the risk is **not accepted**. Only an authorized named owner may accept risk, with rationale, compensating controls, approval date, review/expiry date, and evidence. Blank/TBD owners are themselves a blocker.

Likelihood and impact are preliminary engineering ratings, not formal organizational sign-off.
